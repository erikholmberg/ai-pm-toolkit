# AI Accelerator Onboarding

Help developers get started with AI tools and accelerators effectively.

## Usage

Use these prompts to create onboarding materials, welcome new developers to your AI platform, or help teams adopt AI-assisted development.

---

## AI Adoption Readiness Prompt

```
Help me assess a development team's readiness for AI tool adoption.

## Team Context
- Team size: [NUMBER]
- Primary work: [WHAT THEY BUILD]
- Current tools: [EXISTING TOOLCHAIN]
- AI experience: [NONE/SOME/EXPERIENCED]

## Assessment Areas

### 1. Technical Readiness
- Do they have compatible development environments?
- Are there security/compliance blockers?
- Is their code in a state AI tools can help with?

### 2. Cultural Readiness
- Openness to new tools
- Time available for learning
- Leadership support

### 3. Use Case Fit
- What % of their work could AI accelerate?
- Quick wins available
- Complexity of their domain

### 4. Support Needs
- Training required
- Champion identified?
- Ongoing support model

## Generate:
- Readiness score (1-5)
- Recommended adoption approach
- Potential blockers and mitigations
- Success metrics to track
```

## Tips for AI Accelerator Programs

- **Start with believers** - Early adopters build momentum and case studies
- **Show, don't tell** - Live demos beat documentation
- **Measure productivity, not usage** - Tool adoption isn't the goal; outcomes are
- **Create safe spaces to experiment** - Fear of looking dumb slows adoption
- **Celebrate wins publicly** - Success stories drive more adoption
- **Collect friction feedback** - What's stopping people from using it?
- **Train the trainers** - Create champions in each team
