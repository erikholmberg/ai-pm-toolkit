## Usage

Replace the `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with your specific information.

## Tips

- **Focus on customer value, not features** - Why do customers choose them?
- **Watch their job postings** - Reveals where they're investing
- **Read their reviews** - G2, Capterra, TrustRadius are goldmines
- **Talk to their churned customers** - Best source of weaknesses
- **Monitor their content** - Blog posts reveal strategic direction
- **Don't obsess** - Competitive awareness ≠ competitive paralysis
