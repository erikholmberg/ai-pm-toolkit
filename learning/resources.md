## Learning Path

### Month 1: Foundations
1. Google ML Crash Course
2. Read *Building ML Powered Applications*
3. Subscribe to The Batch and Lenny's Newsletter
4. Play with Claude/ChatGPT daily

### Month 2: Applied Learning
1. DeepLearning.AI short courses
2. Build something with an LLM API
3. Join MLOps Community Slack
4. Start reading AI research summaries

### Month 3: Deep Dive
1. Read *Designing Machine Learning Systems*
2. Full Stack Deep Learning LLM Bootcamp
3. Contribute to your team's AI practices
4. Share what you've learned

## Staying Current

### Weekly Routine
- Read The Batch (10 min)
- Skim r/LocalLLaMA or r/MachineLearning (10 min)
- One podcast episode during commute
- Try one new AI tool or feature

### Monthly Routine
- Deep read of one paper or blog series
- Attend one AI meetup or webinar
- Review and update your learning plan
- Connect with one new person in AI PM
