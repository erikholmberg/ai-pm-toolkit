# AI PM Skills Roadmap

A progressive guide to developing AI Product Management skills.

---

## Skill Levels

### Level 1: AI-Aware PM
*Can work on products that use AI*

### Level 2: AI-Competent PM
*Can own AI features and work effectively with ML teams*

### Level 3: AI-Expert PM
*Can lead AI product strategy and coach others*

---

## Level 1: AI-Aware PM

**Goal:** Understand AI basics, communicate with ML teams, make informed decisions

### Technical Literacy
- [ ] Understand ML basics (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement)
- [ ] Know what LLMs are and how they work at high level
- [ ] Understand key terms (training, inference, accuracy, latency)
- [ ] Know the difference between traditional software and ML

**Resources:**
- Google ML Crash Course (free)
- This toolkit's [AI/ML Fundamentals](../learning/ai-ml-fundamentals-for-pms.md)

### PM Fundamentals Applied to AI
- [ ] Know how to write requirements for ML features
- [ ] Understand that ML features need different success metrics
- [ ] Know to plan for iteration (ML is never "done")
- [ ] Recognize when AI is/isn't the right solution

### Stakeholder Communication
- [ ] Can explain AI features without jargon
- [ ] Can set realistic expectations about AI capabilities
- [ ] Can communicate AI risks appropriately

### Key Questions to Ask
- "What data do we need?"
- "How will we know if it's working?"
- "What happens when it's wrong?"

---

## Level 2: AI-Competent PM

**Goal:** Lead AI features, partner effectively with ML engineers, drive AI product quality

### Technical Understanding
- [ ] Understand model training process at conceptual level
- [ ] Know about data quality, bias, and drift
- [ ] Understand tradeoffs (accuracy vs. latency vs. cost)
- [ ] Familiar with evaluation metrics and their meanings
- [ ] Know about prompt engineering fundamentals

**Resources:**
- *Designing Machine Learning Systems* by Chip Huyen
- *Building Machine Learning Powered Applications* by Emmanuel Ameisen

### Product Management for AI
- [ ] Can define success metrics for ML features
- [ ] Know how to scope ML projects (start small, iterate)
- [ ] Understand data requirements and can advocate for data collection
- [ ] Can plan evaluation strategies
- [ ] Know how to handle ML project uncertainty

### Quality & Safety
- [ ] Understand bias and fairness concepts
- [ ] Can design evaluation approaches
- [ ] Know how to think about AI safety
- [ ] Can plan for graceful degradation

### Cross-Functional Leadership
- [ ] Effective partner to ML engineers
- [ ] Can facilitate tradeoff discussions
- [ ] Can translate between business and technical needs
- [ ] Can manage stakeholder expectations on AI timelines

### Key Activities
- Lead an AI feature from concept to launch
- Define and run an evaluation
- Handle an AI feature incident
- Write an AI feature PRD

---

## Level 3: AI-Expert PM

**Goal:** Shape AI strategy, build AI PM capabilities, push the craft forward

### Strategic Thinking
- [ ] Can develop AI product strategy
- [ ] Understand AI competitive landscape
- [ ] Can identify AI opportunities and threats
- [ ] Know when to build, buy, or partner
- [ ] Can evaluate AI vendors and tools

### Deep Technical Knowledge
- [ ] Familiar with different model architectures and their tradeoffs
- [ ] Understand RAG, fine-tuning, prompt engineering deeply
- [ ] Can evaluate technical approaches
- [ ] Stay current with AI developments

### Organizational Leadership
- [ ] Can build AI PM capabilities in a team
- [ ] Coach other PMs on AI
- [ ] Develop AI PM practices and playbooks
- [ ] Advocate for responsible AI practices

### Industry Engagement
- [ ] Share learnings externally (talks, writing)
- [ ] Build network in AI PM community
- [ ] Contribute to advancing the practice

### Key Activities
- Develop AI product strategy for a product area
- Build evaluation and quality frameworks
- Mentor junior AI PMs
- Influence organizational AI direction

## Skills by Area

### Technical Skills

| Skill | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| ML Fundamentals | Basic understanding | Solid grasp | Deep expertise |
| LLMs/GenAI | Know what they are | Prompt engineering | Architecture decisions |
| Evaluation | Know it's needed | Design and run evals | Build eval frameworks |
| MLOps | Know it exists | Understand workflows | Influence practices |

### Product Skills

| Skill | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| Requirements | Adapt to AI | Write AI-native | Templates and training |
| Success Metrics | Use guidance | Define independently | Create frameworks |
| User Research | Standard research | AI-specific research | Innovate methods |
| Prioritization | Use frameworks | Adapt for AI | Create AI-specific |

### Leadership Skills

| Skill | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| Communication | Explain basics | Manage expectations | Shape narratives |
| Stakeholders | Align | Influence | Evangelize |
| Team | Collaborate | Partner | Coach |
| Strategy | Contribute | Develop | Lead |
